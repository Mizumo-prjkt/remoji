# Microsoft Edge Add-ons: Remoji Emoji Picker Description

## 📝 Short Description (Max 150 characters)
Remoji is a privacy-first, intelligent emoji picker that learns your habits, supports fuzzy search, and features community-driven secret themes.

---

## 📄 Detailed Description

**Tired of slow, clunky emoji pickers? Meet Remoji—the fastest, smartest way to express yourself.**

Remoji isn't just another emoji menu; it’s an intelligent companion that adapts to how you type. Whether you're chatting on Discord, posting on Reddit, or emailing a colleague, Remoji puts your most relevant emojis right at your fingertips.

### 🧠 Why Remoji is Different:

*   **Intelligent Learning Engine**: Remoji tracks which emojis you use most frequently on specific websites. Over time, it builds a personalized "Frequently Used" tab that is hyper-relevant to your actual habits.
*   **Contextual Awareness**: Emojis you use on Reddit might be different from what you use on LinkedIn. Remoji can track usage per domain and even per subreddit, giving you the right suggestions for the right community.
*   **Blazing Fast Fuzzy Search**: Don't waste time scrolling. Use our advanced fuzzy search to find emojis by name, concept, or even internet slang like ":3", "sus", "miku", or "ratio".
*   **Draggable & Non-Intrusive**: Need to see the text behind the picker? Just grab the header and drag it anywhere on your screen. It stays where you put it.
*   **Pinned Mode**: For when you need to send a wall of emojis—click the Pin icon to keep the picker open for multiple selections without it closing.
*   **🎮 Unlockable Easter Eggs**: Discover beautiful, custom-designed CSS themes by visiting your favorite communities (like Discord, Twitter/X, and various subreddits). Track your collection in the Remoji Achievement dashboard!

### 🛡️ Privacy is Our Priority

We believe your typing habits are personal. Unlike other extensions, **Remoji processes everything 100% locally.**
*   NO servers.
*   NO telemetry or tracking.
*   NO data collection.
Your analytics and usage patterns never leave your machine. You can view, manage, or wipe your data at any time through the built-in Admin Console.

---

### 🚀 How to Use:
1.  Click into any text field.
2.  Press **`Ctrl + .`** (Control + Period) to open the picker.
3.  Type to search or click an emoji to insert it instantly.
4.  Click the **Gear (⚙️)** icon to customize your settings and view your achievements.

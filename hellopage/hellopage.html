What's New in Remoji v1.0.4

Here are the latest features and bug fixes built just for you!

💬

Discord Colon Shortcodes (:emoji:)

We've integrated native Discord shortcode mapping! When typing on discord.com, Remoji automatically replaces raw emojis with their colon shortcode format (e.g. :sob: ) to allow Discord to cleanly parse and render them using their high-res custom emojis.

⚡

Fixed Slate.js "Phantom Box" Desync

No more overlapping text or broken editors in rich-text setups. By dispatching standard beforeinput events, Remoji keeps Discord and other advanced Slate.js editor engines perfectly in sync!

🐧

Unlockable Zundamon theme on r/zundamon

Added achievements and styling support for the r/zundamon subreddit. Collect your achievements and view them directly in your Achievements tab.

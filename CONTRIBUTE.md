# Contributing to Remoji Browser Extension

## Submitting a Pull Request

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a branch for your changes.
3. Commit your changes.
4. Push your changes.
5. Open a pull request.

I will study your pull request and respond to you as soon as possible. If there's anything I want to change, I'll let you know in the pull request comments.

## Bug Reports

For bug reports, please open an issue and provide a detailed description of the bug, including steps to reproduce it and any relevant information.

## Feature Requests

For feature requests, please open an issue and provide a detailed description of the feature, including why you think it would be a good addition to the extension and any relevant information.

## Community Guidelines

*   **Be respectful**: Please be respectful of other contributors and their ideas.
*   **Be constructive**: Please provide constructive feedback and suggestions.
*   **Be patient**: Please be patient with the development process and the maintainers.


**This project is not funded in any way, so please do not expect any compensation for your contributions, nor anyone will act immediately on your pull requests, or issues.**
